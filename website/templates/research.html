01 — RESEARCH OVERVIEW

From dataset audit
to model evaluation.

A summary of the experimental workflow and reported held-out test results.

EXPERIMENTAL WORKFLOW

Methodology

The pipeline is designed to examine model performance while reducing duplicate-related data leakage and documenting model behaviour beyond accuracy.

01

Dataset audit & grouping

Image metadata and duplicate/near-duplicate relationships were examined. Related images were grouped to support leakage-controlled splitting.

02

Preprocessing experiments

Raw images and alternative enhancement approaches were compared, including CLAHE, histogram equalization, blur, sharpening and adaptive methods.

03

Deep-learning models

MobileNetV2, EfficientNetB0 and ResNet50 were trained and evaluated. Group-aware five-fold cross-validation was performed on development data.

04

Evaluation & explainability

Held-out test metrics, Grad-CAM visualizations, Monte Carlo Dropout uncertainty estimates and TensorFlow Lite conversion were examined.

03 — ADDITIONAL ANALYSIS

Beyond classification.

Additional experiments help characterize model behaviour, but do not establish clinical reliability.

EXPLAINABILITY

Grad-CAM

Class-activation heatmaps and overlays were generated for selected eligible test images.

UNCERTAINTY

Monte Carlo Dropout

Repeated stochastic forward passes were used to estimate predictive variability and calibration-related summaries.

DEPLOYMENT

TensorFlow Lite

Float16 models were benchmarked for model size, inference latency and agreement with Keras outputs.
