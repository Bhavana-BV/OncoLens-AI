THE PROJECT

Research with evaluation
at its core.

Beyond a single accuracy score, the project examines duplicate-aware data splitting, class-wise performance, calibration, visual explanations and lightweight deployment.

01 / DATA

Leakage-aware dataset

Duplicate and near-duplicate grouping is considered before separating development and held-out test data.

02 / MODELS

Model comparison

MobileNetV2, EfficientNetB0 and ResNet50 are investigated using a consistent research evaluation framework.

03 / TRUST

Interpretability & uncertainty

Grad-CAM and Monte Carlo Dropout provide additional views of model behaviour and predictive uncertainty.
